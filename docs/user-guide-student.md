## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. **Visit the website**: Go to [Platform URL]

## Using Gems for Discounts

### What are Gems?

Gems are virtual currency you can use to get discounts on classes. Think of them as loyalty rewards!

**Gem Value**: 1 Gem = $0.50 discount

**Example**:
- Class costs $20
- You have 30 Gems
- You can save up to $15 (50% of $20 = max discount)
- Final price: $5 + use 30 Gems

---

### Gem Discount Rules

1. **Maximum discount**: 50% of class price
2. **Minimum payment**: You must pay at least $5 in cash/card
3. **Whole gems only**: Can't use partial Gems
4. **Non-refundable**: Once used, Gems are deducted immediately
5. **Gems are refunded** if you cancel within the allowed timeframe

**Screenshot Placeholder**: [Gem Discount Slider]

---

### How to Apply Gems

When booking a class:

1. You'll see the **Gem Discount Slider**
2. Drag the slider or enter the number of Gems
3. The system automatically calculates:
   - Maximum Gems you can use (50% cap)
   - Available Gems in your balance
   - Final price after discount

4. The slider shows:
   - 🟢 **Green zone**: Optimal discount
   - 🔴 **Red zone**: Cannot use more (50% limit reached)

**Pro Tip**: Always use Gems! They expire after 90 days, so use them before they're gone.

### Attendance Streaks

**What is a streak?**
A streak is the number of consecutive days you attend at least one class.

**How it works**:
- Attend a class today: Streak continues ✅
- Miss a day: Streak resets to 0 ❌
- Streaks are calculated at 00:00 UTC daily

**Streak Bonuses**:
- Earn bonus Gems at milestones (7, 14, 30, 60, 90 days)
- Your longest streak is saved as a record
- See your current streak on your dashboard

**Screenshot Placeholder**: [Streak Counter]

**Tips for maintaining streaks**:
- Book classes in advance
- Set daily reminders
- Book multiple classes per week
- Don't let weekends break your streak!

### Cancelling a Booking

**Cancellation Policy**:
- **More than 24 hours before**: Full refund (Gems + payment)
- **12-24 hours before**: 50% refund
- **Less than 12 hours**: No refund (teacher compensation)
- **Teacher cancels**: Always full refund + 50 Gem compensation

**How to cancel**:

1. **Go to "My Bookings"**
2. **Find the class** you want to cancel
3. **Click "Cancel Booking"**
4. **Select cancellation reason**
5. **Confirm cancellation**
6. **Receive refund**:
   - Gems: Instant
   - Payment: 3-5 business days

**Screenshot Placeholder**: [Cancellation Modal]

**Important Notes**:
- ⚠️ Cancelling within 24 hours may result in partial refunds
- ⚠️ Repeated last-minute cancellations may affect your account
- ✅ Cancel early to help teachers plan their schedule

## FAQs

### Account & Registration

**Q: How old do I need to be to register?**
A: You must be at least 13 years old. Users under 18 require parental consent.

**Q: Can I change my email address?**
A: Yes, go to Profile > Settings > Change Email. You'll need to verify the new email.

**Q: I forgot my password, what do I do?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions.

---

### Gems & Discounts

**Q: Do Gems expire?**
A: Yes, Gems expire 90 days after you earn them. You'll receive a reminder 7 days before expiration.

**Q: Can I transfer Gems to another student?**
A: No, Gems are non-transferable and tied to your account.

**Q: What happens to Gems if I cancel a booking?**
A: Gems are refunded to your account based on the cancellation policy.

**Q: Is there a limit to how many Gems I can have?**
A: Yes, maximum balance is 1,000 Gems. Earn more only after spending some.

**Q: Can I buy Gems with money?**
A: No, Gems can only be earned through platform activities.

---

### Classes & Bookings

**Q: Can I book multiple classes at once?**
A: Yes! Browse and book as many as you'd like.

**Q: What if the teacher cancels my class?**
A: You'll receive a full refund plus 50 compensation Gems.

**Q: Can I reschedule a booking?**
A: Not directly. You need to cancel and book a new class (subject to cancellation policy).

**Q: What if I miss a class?**
A: Unfortunately, no-shows are non-refundable. Make sure to cancel in advance if you can't attend.

**Q: Can I attend from my phone?**
A: Yes! Our platform works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

---

### Video Classes

**Q: What do I need for video classes?**
A: A device with camera, microphone, and stable internet connection (minimum 5 Mbps recommended).

**Q: Can I turn off my camera during class?**
A: Teachers may require cameras on for better engagement, but you can mute if needed.

**Q: Are classes recorded?**
A: Only if the teacher enables recording. You'll be notified if recording is active.

**Q: What if I have technical issues during class?**
A: Contact support immediately via the in-class chat or support button.

---

### Payments

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: VNPay, MoMo, ZaloPay (Vietnam), and Credit Cards via Stripe (International).

**Q: Is payment required if I use enough Gems?**
A: No! If your Gems cover at least $5 of the class, you can book for free.

**Q: How long do refunds take?**
A: Gem refunds are instant. Payment refunds take 3-5 business days.

**Q: Are there any fees?**
A: No hidden fees! The price you see is what you pay.

---

### Support

**Q: How do I contact support?**
A: Click the Help icon in the bottom-right corner or email support@platform.com.

**Q: Can I report inappropriate behavior?**
A: Yes, please report immediately via the Report button in any class or message.

**Q: How do I give feedback about a teacher?**
A: After each class, you can rate and review the teacher.
